The Silent Epidemic

Spinal Dysfunction in Working Professionals

Modern work culture—prolonged sitting and poor ergonomics—is driving a surge in "Office Syndrome". Millions suffer from chronic neck and back pain, often seeking care only when conditions are severe, over-relying on medication, with surgery as a last resort and minimal focus on prevention and costly to reverse, creating a massive gap in preventive care.

The Gap: Most patients seek help only when the damage is severe because there hasn't been a reliable way to catch it early—until now.

The Breakthrough Model

Screen → Stratify → Intervene

A standardized, technology-enabled pathway for objective spine care

Step 1

Screen

AI-enabled 2D/3D digital spine and postural scanning for early identification.

Step 2

Stratify

Objective risk classification (Normal to Severe) using functional diagnostics like balance, gait, and muscle strength.

Step 3

Intervene

Structured 20-day rehab programs using US-FDA cleared Robotic Spinal Decompression and evidence-based therapy.

About Us | Why Pro Spine

Our Story

Every great innovation begins with a difficult question. For Dr. Pranav Kumar, after 21 years and 50,000 patients, the question was: Why must patients choose between basic therapy that doesn't work and surgery that scares them?

Pro Spine was born in April 2024 to be that "missing bridge." We found that by combining AI-driven diagnostics with robotic technology, we could restore lives without a single incision. Today, we aren't just treating back pain; we are leading a revolution in non-surgical care.
